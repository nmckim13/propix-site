Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 11, 2025

Pro Pix ("we," "us," or "our") operates the website propix3d.com and provides real estate photography and media services. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, disclose, and protect your personal information when you use our website or services.

By using our website or services, you agree to the collection and use of information as described in this policy.

SMS and Text Message Communications

By providing your phone number and booking our services, you consent to receive SMS text messages from Pro Pix. These messages may include booking confirmations, appointment reminders, shoot day notifications, photo delivery alerts, and follow-up communications related to your order.

Types of SMS Messages We Send

When you provide your mobile phone number to Pro Pix, you may receive the following types of text messages:

  • Booking confirmations — confirmation of your scheduled photography appointment
  • Appointment reminders — reminders sent before your scheduled shoot
  • Shoot day notifications — day-of communications including photographer arrival updates
  • Delivery notifications — alerts when your photos, videos, or other deliverables are ready for download
  • Order updates — status updates on editing, processing, or any changes to your order
  • Follow-up messages — post-delivery check-ins and satisfaction follow-ups
  • Promotional messages — special offers, discounts, or new service announcements (only if you have opted in)

Message Frequency

Message frequency varies based on your booking activity. You may receive up to 5–10 messages per booking. Promotional messages are sent no more than 4 times per month.

How to Opt Out

You may opt out of SMS communications at any time by replying STOP to any text message you receive from us. After opting out, you will receive a single confirmation message and no further SMS messages will be sent unless you re-subscribe.

To re-subscribe after opting out, reply START to any of our messages or contact us directly.

Help

For help with SMS communications, reply HELP to any of our messages or contact us at the information listed below. Message and data rates may apply. Carriers are not liable for delayed or undelivered messages.

Consent is Not Required for Purchase

You are not required to consent to receive promotional SMS messages as a condition of purchasing our services. However, transactional messages (booking confirmations, delivery alerts) are part of our standard service workflow.

Information We Collect

Information You Provide

  • Contact information — name, email address, phone number, and mailing address
  • Booking information — property address, appointment date and time, service selections
  • Payment information — processed securely through our payment processor; we do not store card numbers
  • Communications — messages, emails, or notes you send to us

Information Collected Automatically

  • Usage data — pages visited, time spent, referring URLs, browser type, and device information

How We Use Your Information

We use the information we collect to:

  • Process and fulfill your bookings and orders
  • Send booking confirmations, reminders, and delivery notifications via email and SMS
  • Communicate with you about your orders, questions, or concerns
  • Improve our website, services, and customer experience
  • Send promotional offers and marketing communications (with your consent)
  • Comply with legal obligations
  • Prevent fraud and protect the security of our services

How We Share Your Information

We do not sell your personal information. We may share your information with:

  • Service providers — third-party vendors who help us operate our business (booking platforms, payment processors, SMS providers, email services). These providers are contractually obligated to protect your information.

We do not share your information with third parties for their own marketing purposes.

Data Retention

We retain your personal information for as long as necessary to provide our services and comply with legal obligations. Booking records are typically retained for 3 years. You may request deletion of your data at any time by contacting us.

Your Rights

To exercise any of these rights, contact us at the information below.
